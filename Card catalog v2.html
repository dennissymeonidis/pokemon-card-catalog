Drag cards between pockets, on either page. To move a card to another page, hold it over the arrow at the side of the binder (or scroll the mouse wheel) and the pages turn. Tap an empty pocket (+) to add a card there from a photo, or Scan page to read a photo of a whole page. Drop a picture file from your computer onto a pocket to replace its photo, or onto an empty pocket to add a card. Use Select cards to pick several cards, then drag one of them to move them all.
